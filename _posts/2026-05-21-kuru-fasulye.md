This is a simple recipe from Turkey, and a great comfort food!

Soak some white beans or butter beans overnight. The next day,
pressure cook them and keep the cooking water.

Add in a pot:
  - Sliced onion
  - Sliced garlic
  - Chopped tomatoes (a significant amount)
  - Salt
  - Pepper
  - Paprika
  - Chili powder
  - A bit of dried oregano
  - Some of the bean cooking water until it covers the veggies

Bring to a boil and let it simmer for 20 minutes. Blend this soup and
put it back in the pot. Add the beans and a bit of their cooking
water. Bring to a boil again and let it simmer for another 20 minutes.
The end result should look a bit like a stew.
