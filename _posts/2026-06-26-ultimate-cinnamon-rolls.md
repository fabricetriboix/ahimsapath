I did a lot of experiments, but I just can't find a way to make decent
cinnamon rolls without at least a bit of oil. So here is a recipe that
uses some coconut oil (please feel free to replace it with any other
oil you want), but is still somewhat healthy.

In a big bowl, put the following (and in that order):

 - 1 cup unsweetened soya milk
 - 1.5 cup atta flour
 - 3 tbsp vital wheat gluten
 - 3 tbsp unrefined sugar
 - 2 tsp dry yeast
 - 1/2 tsp salt
 - 1.5 tsp coconut oil

The additional gluten is necessary because atta flour is not strong
enough. It will make the dough more elastic and less of a brick.
Please also note that the salt is kind of necessary to get the yeast
to properly work. Mix the ingredients with your fingers.. Adjust the
milk or the flour so to adjust the consistency. The dough should be
elastic, soft and a bit sticky on your fingers.

Then put the dough on a floured surface and knead it 150 turns. Cover
with a bowl and let it rest for 10 minutes.

Mix 3 tbsp of unrefined sugar with 3 tsp of cinnamon.

Spread the dough with a pin roller into a rectangle shape. Don't roll
it too thin, keep it quite thick. Spread 1 tbsp of coconut oil on top
of it, and then spread the sugar+cinnamon mixture. Then roll the
dough; make sure you don't roll it too tightly.

Cut it into 8 rolls. The best way to do it is to use dental floss:
pass the dental floss under the roll of dough, cross it on top and
then pull on each side. That gives a nice cut.

Put the rolls in an oiled baking tray, cover with cling film and let
it rise for one or two hours, or however long is required for the
rolls to double in size.

Pre-heat the oven at 180C (with fan) and bake the rolls for 20
minutes, then turn the rolls upside down and bake for another 5
minutes.

Delicious!
