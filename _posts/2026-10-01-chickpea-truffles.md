This is a great treat, and super healthy!

Tip in a food processor:
  - 1.5 cup cooked chickpeas
  - 1/4 cup cocoa powder
  - 6 large dates
  - 2 tbsp peanut butter

Blend until smooth and roll into bite size balls. Dip in melted dark
chocolate and let them cool down in the fridge.

That's super easy and super delicious!
